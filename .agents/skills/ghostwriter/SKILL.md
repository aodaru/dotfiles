# Ghostwriter

Professional ghostwriting skill that creates content in a specific voice/style, with automatic grammar/spell checking.

## When to Use This Skill

Activate when user:
- Asks to "escribe como", "ghostwriter", "redacta", "create content"
- Says "write in style of", "write for", "create article as"
- Needs content written in someone else's voice
- Asks for "blog post", "email", "article", "social media post"

## Workflow

### Step 1: Gather Context

Before writing, gather:

1. **Topic/Subject**: What is the content about?
2. **Target Audience**: Who will read it? (experts, beginners, general public)
3. **Tone/Voice**: 
   - Formal or casual?
   - Professional or friendly?
   - Authoritative or conversational?
4. **Purpose**: Inform, persuade, entertain?
5. **Format**: Blog post, article, email, newsletter, social media?
6. **Length**: Word count or estimated read time
7. **Style Reference**: Any existing content from the client to match their voice?

### Step 2: Create Draft

Generate content following:
- Clear structure (introduction, body, conclusion)
- Appropriate tone for audience
- Natural flow and readability
- Compelling headline/title

### Step 3: Apply text-editor (Critical)

**After creating the draft, ALWAYS use text-editor to proofread:**
1. Call the text-editor skill or use it to check the generated content
2. Apply suggested corrections
3. Ensure grammar and spelling are perfect

### Step 4: Deliver Final Content

- Provide the final polished content
- Optionally include:
  - Alternative headlines
  - Key points highlighted
  - Suggestions for images/visuals (if relevant)

## Content Types

### Blog Post / Article
- Compelling headline
- Introduction with hook
- 3-5 main sections
- Practical takeaways
- Call to action

### Email
- Clear subject line
- Greeting
- Purpose stated early
- Concise body
- Professional closing

### Social Media
- Platform-appropriate length
- Engaging hook
- Hashtags if relevant
- Call to action if appropriate

### Newsletter
- Catchy subject line
- Personal greeting
- 3-5 curated items
- Brief descriptions
- Clear CTA

## Tone Guidelines

| Tone | Characteristics | Example |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| Formal | Professional, proper, no slang | "We appreciate your continued support" |
| Casual | Relaxed, friendly, conversational | "Thanks so much for sticking with us!" |
| Authoritative | Confident, expert, direct | "This is the definitive guide to..." |
| Persuasive | Emotional, compelling, action-oriented | "Don't miss this incredible opportunity" |
| Educational | Clear, patient, explanatory | "Let's break this down step by step" |

## Edge Cases

- **No context provided:** Ask clarifying questions before writing
- **Unclear audience:** Suggest options or assume general public
- **Mixed languages:** Use text-editor to handle ES/EN corrections
- **Sensitive content:** Be careful with medical, legal, financial advice

## Related Skills

- **text-editor**: REQUIRED - Use after generating content to proofread and correct errors

## Pro Tips

1. **Match existing content:** If user provides samples, analyze vocabulary, sentence structure, and style before writing
2. **Ask about keywords:** If SEO is important, ask for target keywords
3. **Consider format:** Adjust length and structure based on platform (LinkedIn vs blog vs email)
4. **Always proofread:** The text-editor step is mandatory for professional output
